## AI 蛋白 / 基因组设计 / AI Protein & Genome Design

**Mohammed AlQuraishi / OpenFold3** — 9 月 14 日 Nature 报道：一项联合研究基于 2 万余条制药公司私有蛋白结构对 OpenFold3 进行微调，性能显著超越仅用公开数据训练的模型；AlQuraishi 呼吁建立同等规模的公开数据集 / Nature (Sept 14): a protein AI fine-tuned on 20,000+ pharma-proprietary structures substantially outperforms public-data models; AlQuraishi says "you get a pretty big bump in performance" and calls for public equivalents.
- [Nature news, Sept 15](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02882-x)
- [Positron Today mirror](https://positron.today/posts/2026-09-14-drug-firms-secret-data-supercharge-ai-protein-models/)

**Institute for Protein Design (UW) / David Baker** — 9 月 3 日宣布 AI BioDesign 联盟：UW、Allen Institute、Fred Hutch 三方联合，五年投入 9500 万美元，以 AI+大规模湿实验室构建开放生物设计资源 / Sept. 3: AI BioDesign coalition (UW, Allen Institute, Fred Hutch) launched with $95M over 5 years to build open AI tools and datasets for biological design, led by Nobel laureate David Baker.
- [Life Science WA, Sept 8](https://lifesciencewa.org/2026/09/08/ai-learns-natures-code-allen-institute-uw-and-fred-hutch-launch-95m-open-science-initiative/)
- [IPD announcement](https://www.ipd.uw.edu/2026/09/new-seattle-based-coalition-emerges-to-learn-and-codify-natures-design-rules/)
- [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/allen-institute-uw-and-fred-hutch-launch-95m-open-science-initiative/)

**Alexander Rives / ESMFold2** — 2026 年推出 ESMFold2，无需人类教导的先验知识，可预测蛋白质结构并辅助设计治疗性抗体；模型在数十亿蛋白序列上从头训练 / ESMFold2 (2026): protein language model trained from scratch on billions of sequences predicts structures and designs therapeutic antibodies without human-curated biology.
- [Scientific American on ESMFold2](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/new-protein-folding-ai-vastly-expands-on-alphafolds-efforts/)
