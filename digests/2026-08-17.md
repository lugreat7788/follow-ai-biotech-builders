**Sergey Ovchinnikov** — 在 *Nature* 发表论文：从 NMR 数据库（~10,000 个蛋白）中"缺失"的残基出发，训练深度学习模型预测微秒至毫秒级构象交换动力学，并可预测 NMR 弛豫实验测量的交换速率 / Published in *Nature*: mined ~10,000 BMRB protein records to show that residues absent from NMR chemical shift data predict millisecond conformational exchange; DL models trained on "absences" also predict NMR relaxation-measured exchange rates.
- 🔗 https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10989-4 (Aug 10)
- 🔗 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42427544/

**Mohammed AlQuraishi** — 在 *Cell Systems* 发表 TCR-pMHC 相互作用特异性预测研究，结合机器学习与生物物理模型，对 T 细胞受体识别的解析具有重要价值，直接关联癌症免疫疗法与疫苗设计 / *Cell Systems* paper: "Predicting specificity of TCR-pMHC interactions using machine-learning and biophysical models" — directly relevant to cancer immunotherapy and vaccine design.
- 🔗 https://www.cell.com/cell-systems/abstract/S2405-4712(26)00182-1?rss=yes (Aug 13)

**Eric Topol** — 发布新 Substack 长文《50 岁后记忆为何下滑》，综述颠覆性的脑衰老新研究：多组学研究发现中年海马体存在关键拐点（而非线性衰退），血细胞突变（CHIP）与阿尔茨海默病相关神经病理降低约 50% 相关联 / New Ground Truths essay "Why Our Memory Slips After Age 50": multi-omics studies reveal a midlife hippocampal inflection point (not linear decline); CHIP blood stem-cell mutations linked to ~50% less Alzheimer's neuropathology.
- 🔗 https://erictopol.substack.com/p/why-our-memory-slips-after-age-50 (Aug 12)

**Pranav Rajpurkar** — 在 *Nature Medicine* 发表框架论文《迈向医学 AI 超级智能的测试》，与 Stanford、MGH、Oxford 合作者共同提出正式评估标准，用于判断 AI 系统是否已超越专科医生的临床推理能力 / *Nature Medicine* perspective: "Toward a test of medical AI superintelligence" — formal criteria for evaluating whether an AI system's clinical reasoning surpasses human specialists.
- 🔗 https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04539-8 (Jul 28)

## AI 制药公司 / AI Pharma Companies

**Arc Institute** (Brian Hie 团队) — 联合斯坦福，在 *Science* 发表历史上首批由 AI 从零设计的功能性病毒基因组：使用 Evo 1 & 2 基因组语言模型生成 16 种噬菌体，均与已知天然序列无匹配，组合后可消灭两株耐药大肠杆菌。约翰斯·霍普金斯生物安全学者同期警告"治理框架尚不存在" / Stanford + Arc scientists used genome LMs Evo 1 & 2 to design 16 functional bacteriophages from scratch — none match known natural sequences; the cocktail eliminated antibiotic-resistant *E. coli*. Johns Hopkins biosecurity experts warned "the governance does not exist."
- 🔗 https://thenextweb.com/news/the-governance-does-not-exist-ai-just-designed-16-viruses-from-scratch (Aug 6)
- 🔗 https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ai-just-created-a-virus-not-found-in-nature-and-scientists-are-worried/
