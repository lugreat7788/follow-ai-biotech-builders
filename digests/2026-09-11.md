# AI × 生物医药 Builder 动态摘要 | 2026-09-11

## 概览 / Overview

本周（9 月 7–11 日）最值得关注的事件：Insilico Medicine 的 AI 研发新药 rentosertib 在 *Nature Biotechnology* 发表 Phase IIa 数据，显示可同时逆转六种蛋白质组衰老时钟，是迄今 AI 制药领域最具说服力的抗衰老信号之一；Demis Hassabis 在 G20 创新论坛首次公开发声，描绘 Gemini 作为通用 AI 协调层的愿景；Simon Kohl 的 Latent Labs 推出 Latent-Y 全自动抗体发现平台，从文字提示到可交付序列无需人工干预。

The standout news this week (Sept 7–11): Insilico Medicine published Phase IIa data in *Nature Biotechnology* showing its AI-designed drug rentosertib reversed six independent proteomic aging clocks — one of the most compelling anti-aging signals yet from an AI-discovered drug; Demis Hassabis made his first public appearance post-restructuring at the G20 Innovation Forum, outlining Gemini as a general-purpose AI coordination layer; and Simon Kohl's Latent Labs unveiled Latent-Y, a fully autonomous antibody design platform from text prompt to lab-ready sequences.

## 🧬 AI 蛋白 / 药物设计 | AI Protein & Drug Design

**Alex Zhavoronkov / Insilico Medicine** — AI 设计的特发性肺纤维化候选药 rentosertib（TNIK 抑制剂）的 Phase IIa 试验数据于 9 月 7 日发表于 *Nature Biotechnology*：六种独立蛋白质组衰老时钟均显示治疗组生物年龄下降，安慰剂组几乎无变化。Zhavoronkov 于 9 月 8 日在巴黎索邦大学 Nature "AI 时代的医疗重塑"会议上现场展示结果。研究强调这是早期信号，尚不能证明药物在全身减缓衰老。/ AI-designed IPF drug rentosertib's Phase IIa analysis published Sept 7 in *Nature Biotechnology*: all six proteomic aging clocks showed lower biological age in treated patients vs. placebo. Zhavoronkov presented at the Nature "Redefining Healthcare in the Age of AI" conference at Sorbonne, Paris on Sept 8.
- 来源：[EurekAlert / Nature Biotechnology](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1142848) | [Insilico 新闻稿](https://insilico.com/news/rnt0709261-rentosertib-proteomic-aging-clocks) | [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326932/20260908/insilico-medicine-ai-drug-rentosertib-cuts-biological-age-six-independent-protein-clocks.htm) | [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-07/ai-discovered-drug-reverses-aging-markers-in-study-biotech-says)

**Simon Kohl / Latent Labs** — 推出 Latent-Y 全自动治疗分子发现平台：用户输入文字靶点描述，平台自动完成靶点分析、抗体/酶候选设计、计算验证，直接输出实验可用序列；前作 Latent-X2 已可生成具备成药性和低免疫原性的抗体候选。公司由 DeepMind AlphaFold2 核心成员、pLDDT 置信度系统发明者 Simon Kohl 创立，获 Radical Ventures、Sofinnova 等 $5000 万融资。/ Launched Latent-Y, a fully autonomous drug discovery platform: text prompt → target analysis → candidate design → computational validation → lab-ready sequences, no human in the loop. Latent-X2 already generates antibody candidates with drug-like properties and low immunogenicity from the first generation.
- 来源：[SynBioBeta 2026 Speaker Profile](https://www.syntheticbiologysummit.com/2026-speakers/simon-kohl)

**UW Institute for Protein Design（David Baker 实验室）** — 发布两篇背靠背 *Nature* 论文，系统阐明如何通过编程控制蛋白质准对称（quasi-symmetry）来组装类病毒蛋白纳米笼；新设计的蛋白质笼容积比单一形状蛋白质笼大 2–3 倍，显著提升基因药物递送载量。研究与纽约大学 Langone 健康中心、韩国浦项科技大学联合完成。/ Published two back-to-back *Nature* papers on programming quasi-symmetry in protein self-assembly to build large virus-like nanocages — 2–3× larger than single-shape cages, expanding genetic payload capacity for gene therapy delivery.
- 来源：[UW Medicine Newsroom](https://newsroom.uw.edu/news-releases/programming-proteins-with-viral-geometry) | [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/uw-nobel-winning-lab-creates-blueprints-for-roomier-protein-cages-to-deliver-genetic-medicines/)

**Arc Institute（Patrick Hsu 团队等）** — 2026 年虚拟细胞挑战赛进入全面竞赛阶段：近 300 支参赛队伍已提交初始结果，本届为**零样本**设置（无训练集公开），模型须跨六项指标预测从未在 Challenge 中出现的细胞背景下的基因扰动响应，难度大幅超越上届。/ The 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge is in full swing with nearly 300 teams on the leaderboard; this year is zero-shot (no training set released), requiring prediction across 6 metrics of perturbation responses in never-before-seen cell contexts.

Plus a JACC study showing head CT deep-learning features boost cardiovascular risk prediction (C-index 0.82 vs. 0.75), reclassifying 15.7% of ED patients.
