## 🔬 AI 蛋白与药物设计 / AI Protein & Drug Design

**Recursion Pharmaceuticals + Roche/Genentech** — Roche 于 8 月 5 日宣布将 Recursion AI 平台发现的首个神经退行性疾病靶点推进至联合早期发现阶段。该靶点通过对逾万亿个体外培养神经元的 AI 分析（微胶质细胞图谱）筛选而来，是双方合作潜在 40 个项目中的第一个。/ Roche advanced the first AI-derived neurodegenerative disease target from its Recursion partnership into early discovery on Aug 5. The target was found by AI mining a data map built from over one trillion lab-cultured neurons — the first of up to 40 potential programs.

**Recursion Pharmaceuticals** — Q2 财报同时披露：AI 设计的 PI3Kα H1047R 抑制剂 REC-7735 已获 IND 批准，预计 2026 年下半年启动实体瘤 Phase 1/2 临床试验，从靶点到候选药物仅耗时 10 个月。/ REC-7735, an AI-designed PI3Kα H1047R inhibitor for solid tumors, cleared IND and is set to enter Phase 1/2 in H2 2026 — developed in just 10 months via Recursion's AI-native platform.
- 来源：[GlobeNewsWire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/05/3339126/0/en/recursion-reports-second-quarter-financial-results-genentech-options-first-neuroscience-target-into-early-discovery-program.html)

**Chai Discovery（Joshua Meier）** — 7 月 13 日完成 4 亿美元 C 轮融资，估值 38 亿美元（7 个月内三倍增长），累计融资超 6 亿美元。最新模型 Chai-3 大幅提升抗体命中率与结合亲和力；已与辉瑞、礼来、诺华、argenx 建立合作。/ Raised $400M Series C at $3.8B valuation on Jul 13 — tripling in 7 months and surpassing $600M total. Flagship Chai-3 model improves antibody hit rates and binding affinity; partners include Pfizer, Lilly, Novartis, and argenx.

**Simon Kohl / Latent Labs** — 3 月 23 日发布 Latent-Y，业界首个自主生物制剂设计 AI Agent：从文字指令出发，自动完成靶标分析、候选分子设计和计算验证，全程无人工干预。在 9 个靶点中成功产生 6 个纳摩尔级结合物（成功率 67%），速度比专家估算快 56 倍。/ Launched Latent-Y on Mar 23 — the world's first autonomous biologics design AI agent. From a text prompt, it runs target analysis, candidate design, and computational validation with no human intervention. Confirmed binders against 6 of 9 targets (67%) at single-digit nanomolar affinities, 56x faster than expert estimates.
- 来源：[Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260323538522/en/Latent-Y-The-Autonomous-AI-Agent-for-Drug-Design-at-Scale) | [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/latent-y-the-autonomous-ai-agent-for-drug-design-at-scale) | [Latent Labs](https://www.latentlabs.com/press-release/latent-y/)
