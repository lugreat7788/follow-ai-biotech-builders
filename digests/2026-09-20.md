## 🧬 蛋白质与分子设计 / Protein & Molecular Design

**Chai Discovery** — Chai-2 多模态生成模型实现完全从零设计的抗体命中率达 16%，超越此前方法 >100 倍，将抗体发现周期从数月压缩至数周；公司总融资额已达 $2.25 亿（含 2025 年 12 月 $1.3 亿 B 轮），已与 Eli Lilly 达成战略合作。/ Chai-2 achieves a 16% hit rate in fully de novo antibody design—>100× better than prior methods—compressing discovery from months to weeks; total funding $225M; Eli Lilly collaboration active.
- 来源: [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/chai-discovery-scores-70m-de-novo-antibody-design) | [GEN](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/chais-the-limit-for-ai-antibody-designer-after-130m-series-b-funding/) | [AI Biologics / IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-biologics-design-chai-discovery-eli-lilly)

**Mohammed AlQuraishi / Columbia** — Nature 报道：融合超过 2 万个制药公司私有蛋白结构数据训练的 AI 模型在精度上超过仅使用公开数据的 AlphaFold 类模型，AlQuraishi 是这一工作的核心贡献者，为工业数据共享提供有力论据。/ Nature feature: an AI model trained on 20,000+ pharma-proprietary protein structures outperforms AlphaFold-style public-data models; AlQuraishi is a key contributor, building the case for industrial data sharing.
- 来源: [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02882-x)

**Arc Institute** — 2026 年虚拟细胞挑战赛（第二届）于 8 月 20 日开放，要求参赛者实现跨从未见过的细胞系的零样本 CRISPRi 基因敲低响应预测，$10 万奖金，NVIDIA/10x Genomics/Ultima Genomics 赞助，截至目前已超 1,800 人报名。/ The 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge (round 2) launched Aug 20 with a zero-shot task: predict CRISPRi knockdown responses across unseen cell lines; $100K prize, 1,800+ registered teams, sponsored by NVIDIA, 10x Genomics, and Ultima Genomics.

## 💉 mRNA 与精准医疗 / mRNA & Precision Medicine

**Moderna + Merck** — Phase 3 INTerpath-001 试验：个性化 mRNA 肿瘤疫苗 intismeran（mRNA-4157）联合 Keytruda，在完全切除的 IIB-IV 期黑色素瘤患者中，将复发或死亡风险降低 49%（HR 0.51），远端转移或死亡风险降低 59%（HR 0.41），入组 1,137 例；MRNA 股价 9 月 17 日盘中一度涨逾 100%，有望成为首个获批的 mRNA 癌症疫苗。/ Phase 3 INTerpath-001: intismeran + pembrolizumab cut recurrence/death risk by 49% and distant metastasis by 59% vs. pembro alone in 1,137 resected melanoma patients—the first Phase 3 win for any mRNA cancer vaccine. MRNA surged >100% intraday on Sept 17.
- 来源: [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/19/mrna-cancer-vaccine-trial-melanoma-merck-moderna/) | [Merck PR](https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-announce-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-of-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-met-endpoints-of-recurrence-free-survival-rfs-and-distant-metastasis-free-survival-dmfs-in-patient/) | [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/moderna-merck-cancer-vaccine-landmark-result-melanoma/828238/)
