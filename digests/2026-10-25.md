## AI 蛋白质 / 基因组设计 | Protein & Genome Design

**Institute for Protein Design / UW (David Baker 实验室)** — 10/22 与 NVIDIA 联合发布开源模型 **RFD4-Proteina**，将 RosettaFold Diffusion 与 NVIDIA Proteina-Complexa 融合，计算基准测试设计成功率提升 5-10 倍。同周另有论文展示 AI 设计的「分子剪刀」蛋白，可反复切割阿尔茨海默症、ALS 相关靶标蛋白。/ Released open-source **RFD4-Proteina** (Oct 22) jointly with NVIDIA, fusing RosettaFold Diffusion with Proteina-Complexa; computational benchmarks show 5–10× design success rate gains. Separate paper: AI-engineered "molecular scissors" that repeatedly cleave Alzheimer's and ALS target proteins.
