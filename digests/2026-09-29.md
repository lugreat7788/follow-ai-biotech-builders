## AI 制药 / AI Drug Discovery

## AI 蛋白 / 抗体设计 / AI Protein & Antibody Design

**Chai Discovery** — Chai-3 抗体设计模型发布，实验命中率约 35–40%（较 Chai-2 翻倍），7 月完成 4 亿美元 C 轮融资；与辉瑞、诺华签署平台授权协议，与礼来的合作延续。/ Chai-3 antibody model delivers ~35–40% experimental hit rates (2× its predecessor); company closed a $400M Series C in July, and signed platform licenses with Pfizer and Novartis alongside its ongoing Eli Lilly collaboration.
- https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/14/chai-discovery-nabs-400m-series-c-ai-designed-antibodies-reach-big-pharma/
- https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/generative-antibody-design-chai-3-pfizer

**Institute for Protein Design (David Baker / UW)** — Washington Research Foundation 向 UW 蛋白设计研究所拨款 700 万美元（4 年），用于推进 AI 驱动的酶设计"设计-构建-测试-学习"闭环，项目自 2026 年中期启动。/ The Washington Research Foundation awarded $7M over four years (starting mid-2026) to David Baker's UW IPD for an AI-enabled enzyme design initiative bridging computation to real-world applications.
- https://www.wrfseattle.org/news/washington-research-foundation-awards-7-million-to-uw-institute-for-protein-design-for-ambitious-initiative-to-advance-ai-enabled-enzyme-design/
- https://www.geekwire.com/2026/from-computer-to-lab-to-market-nobel-winner-david-baker-lands-7m-for-new-protein-program/

**Arc Institute** — 发布 2026 虚拟细胞挑战赛，要求参赛模型在从未见过的细胞系上完成零样本基因扰动响应预测，持续推进 Evo 2 基因组基础模型和 State / Stack 虚拟细胞模型系列。/ Arc launched the 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge, requiring zero-shot prediction of gene knockdown responses in unseen cell lines; the institute continues advancing Evo 2 (genomic foundation model) and its State/Stack virtual cell model series.
- https://arcinstitute.org/news/virtual-cell-challenge-2026

**Alexander Rives (CZ Biohub)** — 5 月发布 ESMFold2，在蛋白质相互作用（尤其抗体-抗原复合物）结构预测上超越 AlphaFold3；发布含 68 亿蛋白结构的图谱；可在五个癌症/免疫治疗靶点上设计并验证微蛋白和单链抗体。/ ESMFold2 (released May 2026) outperforms AlphaFold3 on protein-complex structures, especially antibody-antigen; released an atlas of 6.8B proteins; validated miniprotein binders across five cancer/immunology targets.
- https://x.com/alexrives/status/2059611151860683097
- https://biohub.org/news/world-model-of-protein-biology/

## AI 医疗 / AI Medicine & Health

**Eric Topol (Ground Truths)** — 9 月 19 日发文《The Big Holes in Wearable Heart Rate Variability And Readiness Scores》，批评 Apple Watch、Oura、WHOOP 等设备的 HRV 与准备度评分几乎没有临床结局研究支撑，呼吁行业提供循证证据。/ Published Sep 19 on Ground Truths: a sharp critique of consumer wearable HRV and readiness scores (Apple Watch, Oura, WHOOP), finding almost no peer-reviewed outcome studies backing their actionable recommendations.
- https://erictopol.substack.com/p/the-big-holes-in-wearable-heart-rate
- https://www.rapamycin.news/t/the-big-holes-in-wearable-heart-rate-variability-and-readiness-scores-article-eric-topol-19-sep-26/26670

**Pranav Rajpurkar (Harvard / a2z Radiology AI)** — a2z Radiology AI 旗舰产品 a2z-Unified-Triage（腹腔 CT 分诊，7 个病种）获 FDA 清关；Rajpurkar 与 Topol 联名呼吁重新定义临床放射科 AI 集成框架，强调信任与角色分离。/ a2z Radiology AI's flagship product a2z-Unified-Triage received FDA clearance for abdomen/pelvis CT triage across 7 conditions; Rajpurkar and Topol jointly called for rethinking AI-human integration frameworks in radiology.
- https://pranavrajpurkar.com/
- https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/abs/10.1148/radiol.261539
