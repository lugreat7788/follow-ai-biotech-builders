## 🧬 AI 蛋白 / 分子设计 · AI Protein & Molecular Design

**Chai Discovery** — 于 10月9日宣布与 GSK 签订合作协议：GSK 提供靶点面板，Chai 模型无需靶点专属训练即可生成设计，GSK 内部湿实验室对所有测试靶点均验证到结合子，合作随即扩展为全面平台合作。其最新模型 Chai-3 聚焦蛋白结构预测与分子设计，今年还与 BMS、礼来、诺华、辉瑞分别达成类似合作，并于 7月完成 4 亿美元 C 轮（估值 38 亿美元）。/ On Oct 9, Chai Discovery announced a collaboration with GSK after AI-generated binder designs passed GSK's in-house wet-lab evaluation across all tested targets — without target-specific training. The deal adds GSK to Chai's 2026 pharma roster (BMS, Eli Lilly, Novartis, Pfizer). Latest model: Chai-3. July 2026 Series C: $400M at $3.8B valuation.
- 来源 / Source: [BioSpace (Oct 9, 2026)](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/chai-discovery-announces-collaboration-with-gsk-following-successful-wet-lab-evaluation) | [ContractPharma](https://www.contractpharma.com/breaking-news/chai-discovery-gsk-announce-collaboration-following-wet-lab-evaluation/) | [HIT Consultant Series C](https://hitconsultant.net/2026/07/14/chai-discovery-raises-400-million-series-c/)

**Alexander Rives / Biohub** — Biohub 于 5月27日开源发布 ESMFold2 + ESMC + ESM Atlas，涵盖 68 亿蛋白序列、11 亿预测结构；Rives（原 EvolutionaryScale / Meta AI）现任 Biohub 科学负责人。ESMFold2 在抗体-抗原复合物预测和蛋白-蛋白互作预测上声称达到或超越 AlphaFold3，实验室已验证对 5 个癌症/免疫学靶点生成高亲和力结合子。/ Biohub (led in science by Alex Rives, formerly EvolutionaryScale/Meta AI) open-sourced ESMFold2, ESMC, and ESM Atlas on May 27, 2026: 6.8B proteins, 1.1B predicted structures. Claims to meet or exceed AlphaFold3 on antibody-antigen and protein-protein complex benchmarks; lab-validated high-affinity binders against 5 disease targets.
- 来源 / Source: [Biohub announcement](https://biohub.org/news/world-model-of-protein-biology/) | [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/new-protein-folding-ai-vastly-expands-on-alphafolds-efforts/) | [Rosetta Commons summary (Jun 2026)](https://rosettacommons.org/2026/06/09/biohub-releases-open-source-models-that-utilize-evolution-to-design-disease-target-binders/)

**Sergey Ovchinnikov (MIT)** — 2026 年在《Science》上发表论文《Toward life with a 19-amino acid alphabet through generative artificial intelligence design》（Science 392, eaeb5171），探索通过 AI 生成设计缩减氨基酸字母表至 19 种；同年还在 Nature Methods 发表利用 AlphaFold2 配对表征预测小分子结合位点的工作。/ 2026 Science paper: "Toward life with a 19-amino acid alphabet through generative artificial intelligence design" (Science 392, eaeb5171); also a 2026 Nature Methods paper on predicting small-molecule binding sites via AlphaFold2's pair representation.
- 来源 / Source: [MIT Biology profile](https://biology.mit.edu/profile/sergey-ovchinnikov/)

**Institute for Protein Design (Baker Lab, UW)** — 2026 年获 Jen-Hsun & Lori Huang 基金会近 700 万 GPU 小时算力资助，并获 Washington Research Foundation 700 万美元酶工程研究资金，持续推进治疗性蛋白与疫苗研究。/ In 2026, IPD received ~7M GPU hours from the Huang Foundation and a $7M enzyme-engineering grant from the Washington Research Foundation, sustaining therapeutic protein and vaccine programs.
