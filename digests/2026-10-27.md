# AI × 生物医药 Builder 日报 · 2026-10-27

## 概览 / Overview

本周 AI 制药领域资本与技术双线爆发：前 OpenAI 科学负责人 Kevin Weil 携 $215M 创立 Warp Sciences，将 AI+机器人引入药物化学 CRO 赛道；AstraZeneca 与 insitro 签下 $1.5B AI 靶点发现大单，Recursion 完成 $200M 超额认购定增并拿到 Sanofi 里程碑付款。Anthropic IPO 招股书中首次正式描述其制药 R&D 布局，标志着基础模型公司向生物医药纵深渗透进一步公开化。

This week saw parallel capital and tech explosions in AI pharma: ex-OpenAI science lead Kevin Weil launched Warp Sciences with $215M to bring AI+robotics into drug chemistry CRO work; AstraZeneca and insitro inked a $1.5B AI target-discovery deal; Recursion closed an oversubscribed $200M offering with a Sanofi milestone. Anthropic's IPO filing formally disclosed its drug R&D ambitions for the first time, marking a new stage of frontier-model companies entering biopharma.
