# AI × 生物医药 Builder 日报 — 2026-08-26

## 概览 / Overview

本周最大冲击来自两端：**Moderna+Merck 的个性化 mRNA 癌症疫苗在黑色素瘤三期临床宣告成功**，是个性化新抗原疗法史上首个 Phase 3 胜利；与此同时，**Demis Hassabis 正式卸任 Google DeepMind CEO**，转任 DeepMind 董事长及 Alphabet 首席科学家，标志着 AI 领导层的历史性重组。AI 制药公司的融资和合作也在加速——Chai Discovery 以 38 亿美元估值完成 4 亿美元 C 轮，Profluent 拿下礼来 22.5 亿美元战略合作。

This week's top signals came from two directions: **Moderna+Merck's personalized mRNA cancer vaccine succeeded in a Phase 3 melanoma trial** — the first Phase 3 win for personalized neoantigen therapy — while **Demis Hassabis formally stepped down as Google DeepMind CEO**, becoming Chairman and Alphabet's first Chief Scientist. AI pharma deal-making also accelerated: Chai Discovery closed a $400M Series C at a $3.8B valuation, and Profluent landed a $2.25B partnership with Eli Lilly.
