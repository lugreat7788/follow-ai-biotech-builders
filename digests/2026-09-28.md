# AI × 生物医药 Builder 日报 | 2026-09-28

## 概览 / Overview

本周最值得关注：Insilico Medicine 的 AI 设计药物 Rentosertib 正式启动全球首个生成式 AI 设计新药的 III 期临床试验，并同期在 *Nature Biotechnology* 发表逆转生物年龄的数据；Moderna mRNA 个性化癌症疫苗 Phase III 黑色素瘤数据将作为晚报摘要亮相 ESMO 2026，带动股价飙升；UW 蛋白质设计研究所联合 Allen Institute 和 Fred Hutch 正式宣布启动 $95M 的 AI BioDesign 开放科研联盟。

> The week's top headlines: Insilico's AI-designed drug rentosertib enters the world's first Phase III trial for a generative-AI-discovered molecule; Moderna's mRNA cancer vaccine data is accepted as a late-breaker at ESMO 2026 after a landmark Phase III melanoma win; and UW's Institute for Protein Design launches a $95M AI BioDesign coalition with Allen Institute and Fred Hutch.

## AI 蛋白质 / 基因组设计 | AI Protein & Genome Design

**Institute for Protein Design (UW / David Baker)** — 9 月 3 日宣布成立 AI BioDesign 联盟（$95M），联合 Allen Institute 和 Fred Hutch，旨在通过 AI + 高通量湿实验室构建开放生物设计资源。/ Launched the $95M AI BioDesign coalition on Sept 3, pairing UW, the Allen Institute, and Fred Hutch to build open AI-powered biological design tools at scale.
- 来源: [Axios Seattle](https://www.axios.com/local/seattle/2026/09/03/seattle-ai-biodesign-allen-institute-uw-fred-hutch-proteins) | [UW IPD](https://www.ipd.uw.edu/2026/09/new-seattle-based-coalition-emerges-to-learn-and-codify-natures-design-rules/)

**Patrick Hsu (Arc Institute / Stanford)** — 荣获 2026 年瑞士最高科研奖 Marcel Benoist Prize，表彰其在 CRISPR 及 RNA 引导系统（包括 Bridge RNA 和 Evo 基因组基础模型）的突破性贡献。/ Awarded the 2026 Marcel Benoist Prize—Switzerland's highest research honor—for his work on CRISPR, bridge RNA, and the Evo genome foundation model.
- 来源: [Wikipedia / Patrick Hsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Hsu) | [Ground Truths profile](https://erictopol.substack.com/p/patrick-hsu-a-trailblazer-in-digital)

**Alexander Rives (Biohub)** — Biohub 发布蛋白质生物学「世界模型」ESMFold2 及含 68 亿序列、11 亿预测结构的 ESM Atlas（5 月），规模超越 AlphaFold 数据库逾 8 亿条目，并已用于设计新型抗体。/ Biohub released ESMFold2 and the ESM Atlas (6.8B sequences, 1.1B structures) in May—surpassing AlphaFold DB by 800M+ entries—and demonstrated de novo antibody design for cancer and immune targets.
- 来源: [Biohub announcement](https://biohub.org/news/world-model-of-protein-biology/) | [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/new-protein-folding-ai-vastly-expands-on-alphafolds-efforts/) | [Latent Space writeup](https://www.latent.space/p/esmfold2)

**Sergey Ovchinnikov (MIT)** — 7 月发表 MSA Pairformer，一个专为蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用协同进化建模而设计的紧凑型蛋白质语言模型。/ Published MSA Pairformer in July, a compact protein language model for modeling coevolution in protein-protein interactions.
- 来源: [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/@sergey-ovchinnikov)

## AI 制药公司 | AI-Pharma Companies

**Insilico Medicine / Alex Zhavoronkov** — 【重磅】9 月 9–10 日，Rentosertib（ISM001-055，TNIK 抑制剂）在北京协和医院和上海肺科医院同日完成首例患者入组，启动 GENESIS-IPF-3 三期临床——这是全球首个进入 Phase III 的生成式 AI 设计创新药。同期，*Nature Biotechnology* 发表数据显示该药在 42 名 IPF 患者中逆转了六种蛋白组学衰老时钟的预测年龄，并于 9 月 8 日在巴黎索邦大学「AI 时代医疗」会议上报告。/ **Major milestone**: Rentosertib, the world's first generative-AI-designed drug, dosed its first Phase III patient on Sept 9–10 (GENESIS-IPF-3). A simultaneous *Nature Biotechnology* paper showed it reversed biological age across six proteomic aging clocks in IPF patients.
- 来源: [Insilico PR](https://insilico.com/news/isn1009261-insilico-medicine-doses-first-patient-genesis-ipf-3) | [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/insilico-medicine-doses-first-patient-in-genesis-ipf-3-the-worlds-first-phase-iii-trial-of-a-generative-ai-driven-innovative-drug) | [Nature Biotech PR](https://insilico.com/news/rnt0709261-rentosertib-proteomic-aging-clocks) | [Fortune profile (Sept 18)](https://fortune.com/2026/09/18/insilico-medicine-bets-china-and-ai-can-deliver-the-drug-industry-next-breakthrough/)

**Moderna** — 9 月 21 日，Moderna 宣布三项 mRNA 个性化新抗原疗法 intismeran autogene 的晚报摘要获 ESMO 2026 大会接受（10 月 23–27 日马德里），股价单日涨逾 13%。此前 8 月 19 日其与默沙东的 Phase III 黑色素瘤 RCT 已宣告成功。/ On Sept 21, Moderna announced three late-breaking ESMO 2026 abstracts on intismeran autogene (mRNA neoantigen therapy), pushing shares up 13%+ following the August Phase III melanoma win with Merck.
- 来源: [BioSpace ESMO PR](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/moderna-announces-late-breaking-data-to-be-presented-at-esmo-congress-2026) | [STAT News Phase III](https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/19/mrna-cancer-vaccine-trial-melanoma-merck-moderna/) | [Trefis Sept 22 analysis](https://www.trefis.com/stock/mrna/articles/616141/was-moderna-stocks-cancer-vaccine-run-visible-in-advance/2026-09-22)

**Profluent / Ali Madani** — 4 月与礼来签订最高 $22.5 亿合作协议，利用 ProGen3 等 AI 模型设计定制位点特异性重组酶用于遗传病治疗；并在 *Nature Biotechnology* 发表 Protein2PAM——一款无需迭代实验室筛选即可快速设计新型 PAM 识别 CRISPR 编辑器的 AI 模型。/ Closed a $2.25B deal with Eli Lilly in April for AI-designed recombinases for genetic medicine; also published Protein2PAM in *Nature Biotechnology* enabling rapid CRISPR editor design without iterative screening.
- 来源: [BusinessWire deal PR](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260428698315/en/Profluent-Announces-Strategic-Partnership-with-Lilly-to-Develop-AI-Designed-Recombinases-for-Genetic-Medicine) | [CRISPR Medicine Protein2PAM](https://crisprmedicinenews.com/press-release-service/card/profluent-demonstrates-ai-can-engineer-protein-dna-interactions-without-iterative-laboratory-screeni/)

**Recursion Pharmaceuticals** — Q2 2026 财报（8 月）更新显示 REC-1245 初步安全性和 PK 数据正面，REC-4539 完成首例患者给药；与 Sanofi 合作第五个 milestone 完成。/ Q2 2026 update: positive initial safety/PK for REC-1245, first patient dosed in REC-4539, and fifth Sanofi milestone achieved.
- 来源: [SEC 8-K Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001601830/000160183026000097/exhibit991-q0226.htm) | [Yahoo Finance analysis](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/healthcare/articles/recursion-ai-drug-pipeline-progress-070832635.html)

- 来源: [Training Data podcast (Sequoia)](https://inferencebysequoia.substack.com/s/training-data-podcast) | [Chai Discovery / Eli Lilly](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-biologics-design-chai-discovery-eli-lilly)
