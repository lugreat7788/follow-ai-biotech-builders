## AI 蛋白质 / 基因组设计 / AI Protein & Genomic Design

**Sergey Ovchinnikov（MIT）** — 在 *Science*（392, eaeb5171）发表论文《Toward life with a 19-amino acid alphabet through generative artificial intelligence design》，首次用 AI 设计出以 19 种氨基酸（剔除一种天然氨基酸）即可正常折叠并发挥功能的蛋白质，挑战"自然生命必须依赖全部 20 种氨基酸"的经典认知。 / Published in *Science* (392, eaeb5171): "Toward life with a 19-amino acid alphabet through generative AI design" — first demonstration that AI-designed proteins can fold and function with only 19 canonical amino acids, challenging a fundamental assumption about life.
- 来源：[MIT Biology 教授主页](https://biology.mit.edu/profile/sergey-ovchinnikov/)

**Arc Institute（Patrick Hsu）** — 宣布 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge：零样本预测 CRISPR 干扰（CRISPRi）knockdown 在 6 个细胞系中的转录组响应，数据集规模远超 2025 年版，大奖 $100K，由 NVIDIA、10x Genomics、Ultima Genomics 赞助。 / Launched the 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge: zero-shot prediction of CRISPRi knockdown transcriptomic responses across 6 unseen cell lines. $100K grand prize sponsored by NVIDIA, 10x Genomics & Ultima Genomics.
- 来源：[Arc Institute 官网](https://arcinstitute.org/news/virtual-cell-challenge-2026) · [BioPharma Trend](https://www.biopharmatrend.com/news/arc-institute-launches-virtual-cell-challenge-1307/)
