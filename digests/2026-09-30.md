## AI 蛋白 / 抗体设计 / AI Protein & Antibody Design

**Mohammed AlQuraishi（Columbia University）** — 9 月 16 日在西蒙斯基金会以"AlphaFold 如何以及学习了什么蛋白结构"为题发表主席公开讲座，深入分析 AlphaFold 的学习机理与局限性，属年度重要科普学术报告。/ Delivered a Simons Foundation Presidential Lecture on Sep 16 titled "How, and What, Does AlphaFold Learn About Protein Structure?", offering a mechanistic analysis of what modern structure-prediction models do—and don't—learn from data.
- https://www.simonsfoundation.org/event/how-and-what-does-alphafold-learn-about-protein-structure/

**Sergey Ovchinnikov（MIT）** — 实验室近期工作：MSA Pairformer（蛋白质间协进化建模语言模型）于 6–7 月发表于 Cell，开源代码与 Colab 供即时实验；此外，实验室成员 Jordan Hoff 借助生成式 AI 设计可抵御柑橘黄龙病的蛋白结合剂，成果被 MIT 生成 AI 影响联盟推介。/ MSA Pairformer (a compact protein-language model capturing coevolution of interacting proteins) was published in Cell in June–July 2026 with open-source code; lab member Jordan Hoff is using generative AI to design protein binders against citrus greening disease.
- https://solab.org/
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LqEi4gjOfCw

**Arc Institute** — 2026 虚拟细胞挑战赛进入中期：已逾 1800 名参赛者注册，排行榜持续更新，总奖金 17.5 万美元（冠军 10 万美元）；本届任务为"零样本"——不提供训练集，模型须预测从未见过的 6 个细胞系的 CRISPRi 基因敲低响应；最终测试集将于 10 月 22 日发布。/ The 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge (launched Aug 20) is now mid-run with 1,800+ registered teams and an active leaderboard; the zero-shot task requires predicting CRISPRi knockdown responses in 6 unseen cell lines; the final test set drops Oct 22. Total prizes: $175K.
- https://arcinstitute.org/news/virtual-cell-challenge-2026
- https://x.com/arcinstitute/status/2090492646917861639

**Institute for Protein Design (UW / David Baker)** — Washington Research Foundation 向 David Baker 实验室拨款 700 万美元（4 年，2026 年中启动），聚焦 AI 驱动的酶设计"从计算到实验室再到市场"闭环，推动诺贝尔奖成果转化。/ The Washington Research Foundation awarded David Baker's UW lab $7M over four years (starting mid-2026) to advance AI-enabled enzyme design from computation to real-world applications, translating Nobel-winning protein design research.

## AI 医疗 / AI Medicine & Health

**Eric Topol（Ground Truths）** — 持续关注医疗 AI 循证问题：9 月 19 日发文《可穿戴 HRV 与准备度评分的大漏洞》，批评消费级可穿戴设备的 HRV/准备度推断几乎没有临床结局研究支撑；同期接受采访强调医疗 AI 产品应以临床结局（而非基准测试表现）为证明标准。/ Published "The Big Holes in Wearable HRV and Readiness Scores" on Sep 19, finding consumer wearables (Apple Watch, Oura, WHOOP) lack peer-reviewed outcome evidence for their HRV/readiness recommendations; continues to call for clinical-outcome proof rather than benchmark performance.
- https://www.100news.tv/2026/09/eric-topol-doctor-who-demands-evidence.html

**Moderna** — 9 月，个性化 mRNA 癌症疫苗 intismeran autogene（联合 Keytruda）的 III 期临床 INTerpath-001 已披露满足主要终点（RFS）和次要终点（DMFS），详细数据将在 10 月 24 日 ESMO 年会正式发布；这是第一个在随机对照 III 期试验中成功的个性化新抗原疫苗。/ The Phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial of intismeran autogene + Keytruda (personalized neoantigen mRNA vaccine) met both RFS and DMFS endpoints in resected Stage IIB–IV melanoma; full data will be presented at ESMO on Oct 24—the first positive Ph3 result for a personalized neoantigen vaccine.
- https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/19/mrna-cancer-vaccine-trial-melanoma-merck-moderna/
- https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/merck-and-modernas-personalized-cancer-vaccine-slows-recurrence-ph-3-trial

**Pranav Rajpurkar（Harvard / a2z Radiology AI）** — 实验室发布 ReXGrounding 挑战赛（在 3492 例专家标注胸部 CT 上将放射报告文本与 3D CT 分割对应），结果将在 MICCAI 2026 呈现；同期发表于 Nature Biotechnology 的观点文章指出下一前沿是跨生物学层次运作的通用生物 AI，而非更优的单任务模型。/ The Rajpurkar lab launched the ReXGrounding Challenge (grounding radiology text to 3D CT segmentations on 3,492 chest CTs) with results at MICCAI 2026; a Nature Biotechnology perspective argues the next frontier is generalist AI spanning the full stack of biology, not better single-task models.
- https://rajpurkarlab.hms.harvard.edu/news/
