## AI 治理 / AI Governance

**Demis Hassabis（Google DeepMind）** — 7 月 14 日发表文章，提议建立美国主导的前沿 AI 标准机构，参照金融业监管机构 FINRA 模式：行业资助、政府监督、前沿模型发布前须经测试（最长 30 天）。OpenAI 的 Sam Altman 和 Elon Musk 均表示支持，白宫正积极审议基于该模板的提案。/ On July 14, Hassabis published a framework proposing a U.S.-led Frontier AI Standards Body modeled on FINRA — industry-funded, government-overseen, with mandatory pre-release testing of frontier models; OpenAI's Altman and Elon Musk have endorsed it and the White House is reviewing a proposal built on this template.

## 蛋白设计数据基础设施 / Protein Design Data Infrastructure

**Stef van Grieken / Cradle** — 7 月 22 日，Cradle 作为创始成员加入 A-Alpha Bio 的 Atlas 联盟，与 GSK、Boltz、Dyno Therapeutics 共建行业首个蛋白工程实验数据共享体系；加入后每年可获约 2,800 万项抗体亲和力测量数据，Grieken 强调"覆盖完整设计周期的实验数据是 AI 蛋白工程的瓶颈"。/ On July 22, Cradle joined A-Alpha Bio's Atlas Consortium as a founding member alongside GSK, Boltz, and Dyno Therapeutics — an industry-first data-sharing initiative; founding members each receive ~28M affinity measurements per year. Grieken noted "data spanning the full design lifecycle" is the core bottleneck for AI protein engineering.
- [GlobeNewswire – Consortium launch](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/22/3331237/0/en/A-Alpha-Bio-Announces-Industry-First-Data-Consortium-to-Solve-AI-Protein-Engineering-s-Experimental-Data-Bottleneck-Founding-members-include-GSK-Boltz-Cradle-and-Dyno-Therapeutics.html)
- [GlobeNewswire – Atlas platform](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/07/3323508/0/en/A-Alpha-Bio-Launches-Atlas-to-Address-the-Data-Bottleneck-in-AI-Enabled-Protein-Design.html)

**Simon Kohl / Latent Labs** — 前 DeepMind AlphaFold2 核心科学家（AlphaFold pLDDT 置信度系统发明者）创立 Latent Labs，推出 Latent-Y 全自动蛋白设计平台：从文本描述到靶点分析、候选序列设计、计算验证全自动完成，在 9 个靶点中 6 个确认结合（成功率 67%，单位数纳摩尔亲和力），比人工估算快 56 倍；已获 Radical Ventures 等 5,000 万美元融资。/ Former DeepMind AlphaFold2 scientist (inventor of pLDDT) founded Latent Labs and launched Latent-Y, a fully autonomous protein design platform achieving a 67% success rate across 9 targets at single-digit nanomolar affinities, 56× faster than expert estimates; backed by $50M from Radical Ventures, Sofinnova, Jeff Dean, and Dario Amodei.

## 前沿蛋白/基因组模型 / Frontier Models

**Alexander Rives / Chan Zuckerberg Biohub** — 近期发布 ESMFold2，在地球全生态元基因组数据上训练，展示清晰的 scaling law，无需人工注入生物学知识即可预测蛋白结构并设计治疗性肿瘤结合体；CZ Biohub 与 Meta 开源了 ESMC（60 亿参数），在抗体预测上超越 AlphaFold。/ Released ESMFold2, a protein language model trained on metagenomic data across Earth's biomes exhibiting clean scaling laws; together with Meta's open-source ESMC (6B parameters) it outperforms AlphaFold on antibodies and has designed therapeutic-grade cancer binders.
- [Latent Space podcast write-up](https://www.latent.space/p/esmfold2)
- [AINEXT](https://ain3xt.com/en/posts/20260530-esmfold2-bitter-lesson-protein-biology/)

**Sergey Ovchinnikov / MIT** — 近期（约 6 月）在 Cell 发表 MSA Pairformer，一个基于多序列比对的紧凑蛋白语言模型，捕捉蛋白相互作用的共进化信息，同步开源代码和 Colab 便于快速实验。/ Recently published MSA Pairformer in Cell — a compact MSA-based protein language model capturing coevolution of interacting proteins, released with open-source code and a Colab for quick experimentation.
- [solab.org](https://solab.org/)
