## 🔬 AI 蛋白 / 基因组设计 | AI Protein & Genome Design

**Institute for Protein Design (UW)** — 发布 WRAP（Water-soluble Rosetta Fold-diffused Amphipathic Proteins）技术，可将原本难以溶于水的跨膜蛋白包裹并稳定于水溶液中，无需去垢剂，相关论文 7 月发表于 *Science*。/ Computationally designed WRAPs wrap around membrane proteins, rendering them water-soluble without detergents while preserving native structure; published in *Science* in July 2026.
- 来源：[A WRAP for biology's greasiest problem – Baker Lab](https://www.bakerlab.org/2026/07/02/a-wrap-for-biologys-greasiest-problem/) | [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1133699)

**Sergey Ovchinnikov (MIT solab)** — MSA Pairformer 论文 6 月发表于 *Cell*，提出一种紧凑型蛋白语言模型，通过多序列比对捕捉蛋白质互作中的协同进化信号，并附开源代码和 Colab 工具。/ The Ovchinnikov lab published MSA Pairformer in *Cell* (June 2026): a compact, MSA-based protein language model capturing coevolution of interacting proteins, with open-source code and Colab.
- 来源：[solab.org](https://solab.org/)

**Alexander Rives (CZ Biohub)** — Chan Zuckerberg Biohub 宣布「虚拟生物学计划」（Virtual Biology Initiative），总投入 5 亿美元（其中 4 亿用于内部研发，1 亿支持外部数据生成团队），同时发布 ESMFold2 蛋白世界模型。/ CZ Biohub announced the Virtual Biology Initiative ($500M total; $400M in-house, $100M external), releasing ESMFold2 — a protein world model that predicts structure and designs therapeutic antibodies without human-taught biology.
- 来源：[Biohub releases protein biology world model – GEN](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/biohub-releases-protein-biology-world-model-to-address-disease/) | [ESM: The Bitter Lesson is Coming for Proteins – Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/esmfold2)

**Arc Institute** — 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge 将于 8 月 20 日正式启动，这是 Arc 第二届全球赛事，评估各团队的虚拟细胞模型能否准确预测细胞在新上下文中的基因活性变化；Patrick Hsu 所在的 Brian Hie Lab 的 Proto 团队亦刚参加了 re:AGENT hackathon（8/15–16，旧金山）。/ The 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge launches August 20 — Arc's second annual global competition evaluating virtual cell models on predicting gene-activity changes in novel contexts.
- 来源：[Arc Institute launches Virtual Cell Challenge – GEN](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/arc-institute-launches-virtual-cell-challenge-to-accelerate-ai-model-development/) | [virtualcellchallenge.org](https://virtualcellchallenge.org/)
