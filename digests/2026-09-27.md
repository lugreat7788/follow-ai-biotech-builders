**Alexander Rives / ESMFold2 (CZ Biohub)** — Rives 团队发布 ESMFold2，在蛋白质复合物结构预测上超越 AlphaFold3，配套发布含 68 亿蛋白质的结构图谱；已被用于设计靶向癌症与免疫疾病相关蛋白的新型抗体，湿实验验证显示高成功率。
ESMFold2 surpasses AlphaFold3 on protein complex prediction, includes an atlas of 6.8B proteins, and has already powered successful de novo antibody designs against cancer-linked proteins.
