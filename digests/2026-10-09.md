## AI 制药公司动态 / AI Pharma Company News

**Isomorphic Labs（Demis Hassabis 领导，Alphabet 旗下）**
— 据彭博社 10 月 8 日报道，Isomorphic Labs 正就新一轮融资进行谈判，目标估值 400–500 亿美元；该公司在 2026 年 5 月已完成 21 亿美元 B 轮，目前推进首个 AI 设计药物于 2026 年底入组临床试验。
— Bloomberg reports (Oct 8) that Isomorphic Labs is seeking a new funding round at a $40–50B valuation; the company closed a $2.1B Series B in May 2026 and is targeting its first AI-designed clinical candidate by end-2026.
🔗 https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/08/alphabet-spinoff-isomorphic-labs-reportedly-raising-funding-at-up-to-50b-valuation/

**Recursion Pharmaceuticals**
— 公司二季度报告（2026 年 8 月）确认 REC-7735（PI3Kα H1047R 突变抑制剂）ZINNIA 一/二期试验将于 2026 年下半年启动；REC-4881 在 FAP 中的额外二期数据将于 2026 年 11 月 CGA-IGC 大会公布。
— Q2 2026 earnings confirm ZINNIA Phase 1/2 trial for REC-7735 (PI3Kα H1047R mutant inhibitor) on track to start in H2 2026; additional Phase 2 data for REC-4881 (MEK1/2i in FAP) expected at CGA-IGC November 2026.
🔗 https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/recursion-pharmaceuticals-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-08-05/

**Insilico Medicine / Alex Zhavoronkov**
— 9 月 7 日《Nature Biotechnology》发表 rentosertib IPF 二期 a 临床再分析：6 种蛋白质组衰老时钟均显示治疗组生物学年龄显著下降（部分时钟估算年轻约 3–6 岁），Topol 等专家认为"鼓舞人心，但需在健康人群中进一步验证"。
— Nature Biotechnology (Sep 7): reanalysis of rentosertib Phase IIa in IPF finds reductions in biological age across 6 proteomic aging clocks; exploratory but first such head-to-head clock comparison in a drug trial; experts call it promising but preliminary.
🔗 https://www.therundown.ai/news/insilico-rentosertib-aging-clocks-ai-drug

## AI 蛋白 / 基因编辑 / Drug Design

**Anthropic — AI 发现类 CRISPR 酶系统（ART）**
— 9 月 24 日 Anthropic 宣布其 Claude 代理自主发现了一种新型酶系统（ART，阵列关联逆转录酶），可能具有类似 CRISPR 的基因编辑潜力。CNN 等媒体于 10 月 8 日持续跟进争议：结果以预印本形式发布，尚未同行评审；斯坦福大学同期独立发现了相似但有区别的系统。CRISPR 先驱 Feng Zhang 称该发现"确实有趣，值得深入研究"。
— Anthropic's Claude AI agents autonomously discovered a CRISPR-like enzyme system (ART) using ~950 agents scanning a DNA database (Sep 24, preprint); ongoing scientific debate continues (CNN, Oct 8). Stanford independently found a similar system. CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang called it "genuinely intriguing."
🔗 https://smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/anthropic-says-its-ai-discovered-a-new-enzyme-system-that-resembles-the-revolutionary-gene-editing-tool-crispr-180989578/
🔗 https://cnn.com/2026/10/08/science/ai-biology-anthropic-dna-discovery

**Anthropic × Adaptyv Bio 蛋白设计大赛（进行中）**
— 第 2 轮挑战（10 月 5–11 日）正在进行中，共 5 个挑战覆盖跨物种反应性、GPCR 等靶点，总奖池超 100 万美元（含 Claude 算力积分）。此前 Anthropic 在 8 月公布的自主设计结果中，1,320 个设计中有 354 个结合靶点，命中率 22–35%，大幅超越行业基准 10–15%。
— Challenge 2 (Oct 5–11) of the Anthropic × Adaptyv protein design competition is ongoing; 5 challenges covering cross-species reactivity and GPCRs, $1M+ prize pool. Anthropic's August binder campaign showed 354/1,320 designs confirmed as binders (22–35% hit rate vs. 10–15% industry baseline).
🔗 https://proteinbase.com/competitions/anthropic-adaptyv-2026
🔗 https://oncodaily.com/technology/anthropic570775

**Profluent / Ali Madani — Eli Lilly 合作**
— 4 月 28 日 Profluent 与礼来签署价值最高 22.5 亿美元的战略合作，共同开发 AI 设计的位点特异性重组酶，用于插入千碱基级 DNA 片段，靶向囊性纤维化、遗传性听力损失等遗传病。
— Profluent signed a deal with Eli Lilly (Apr 28, up to $2.25B) to develop AI-designed site-specific recombinases capable of inserting kilobase-scale DNA, targeting genetic diseases like cystic fibrosis.
🔗 https://www.ropesgray.com/en/news-and-events/news/2026/04/profluent-enters-strategic-partnership-with-lilly-to-develop-ai-designed-recombinases-for-genetic

**Mohammed AlQuraishi — OpenFold3 在 AIandCompBio26**
— AlQuraishi（哥伦比亚大学）将在 2026 年鲁汶 AIandCompBio26 会议上发表 OpenFold3 演讲，摘要截止日期为 10 月 7 日。OpenFold3 是开源的生物大分子结构预测框架，允许研究者在自有数据上微调并检视内部机制。
— AlQuraishi (Columbia) is presenting OpenFold3 at AIandCompBio26 in Leuven; abstract deadline was Oct 7. OpenFold3 is the open-source alternative to AlphaFold3 that researchers can fine-tune and inspect on their own data.
🔗 https://be.linkedin.com/in/wannes-hermans

## 数据 / 基础设施 / Data & Infrastructure

**Google、Meta 等联手投入约 20 亿美元开放生物数据**
— 据 Digitimes 10 月 8 日报道，由 Google、Meta 等约 25 家公司组成的联盟共同承诺近 20 亿美元，用于构建 AI 生物学开放数据基础设施，目标是为细胞/疾病建模提供大规模共享数据集。
— A coalition of ~25 companies including Google and Meta committed ~$2B to open AI biology data infrastructure (Oct 8), aiming to build a shared dataset foundation for biological modeling and drug discovery.
🔗 https://digitimes.com/news/a20261008PR200/google-meta-data-funding-partnership.html
