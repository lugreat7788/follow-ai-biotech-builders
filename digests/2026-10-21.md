## AI 蛋白 / 基因组设计 · AI Protein & Genome Design

**Institute for Protein Design (UW Baker Lab)** — 10月14日在 *Nature* 发表两篇论文，报告从头设计的蛋白酶可在指定位点切割与 TDP-43、淀粉样蛋白-β 等疾病相关的肽链；同日，RFdiffusion3 发表于 *Science*，能在原子级别设计蛋白与核酸，并已生成可降解有机磷毒物的功能性酶。

David Baker's lab published two protease papers in *Nature* (Oct 14) and RFdiffusion3 in *Science* (Oct 15), the latter generating enzymes from scratch that degrade toxic organophosphates and designing protein-nucleic acid systems at atomic resolution.

- 蛋白酶论文报道: [Unite.ai — Baker Lab Designs AI Proteases That Cleave Disease-Linked Proteins](https://unite.ai/baker-lab-designs-ai-proteases-that-cleave-disease-linked-proteins/)
- RFdiffusion3 报道: [GEN — RFdiffusion3 Now Open Source](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/rfdiffusion3-now-open-source-designs-dna-binders-and-advanced-enzymes)
- Baker Lab $700万新资助: [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/from-computer-to-lab-to-market-nobel-winner-david-baker-lands-7m-for-new-protein-program/)

**Mohammed AlQuraishi (Columbia / OpenFold)** — OpenFold 联合体在 2026 年继续推进 OpenFold3，引入"联邦训练"机制让五家药企在不共享私有数据的前提下共同训练模型；其团队还开发了 ROCKET，使用实验数据精化蛋白动态结构（5月新闻，持续推进中）。

The OpenFold3 Federated Initiative now lets five pharma companies train a shared model on proprietary data while keeping it confidential; ROCKET refines protein dynamics from experimental data.

- OpenFold3 报道: [ScienceNews](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/ai-model-openfold3-protein-predictions)
- ROCKET: [Columbia Systems Biology](https://systemsbiology.columbia.edu/news/mohammed-alquraishi-phd-and-collaborators-developed-rcoket)

## AI 制药公司 · AI Pharma Companies

**Recursion Pharmaceuticals** — 10月15日宣布 Sanofi 从双方研发合作中 option 了首个免疫学候选药物，触发约 1000 万美元里程碑付款，该药物计划2027年启动 Phase 1；同周完成 2 亿美元增发（每股3.25美元，约 6150 万股），9月30日账面现金约 5.61 亿美元。

Sanofi exercised an option on Recursion's first collaboration immunology development candidate (Oct 15), triggering ~$10M milestone; Recursion simultaneously priced a $200M oversubscribed follow-on offering closing ~Oct 16.

**Chai Discovery** — 10月9日宣布与 GSK 建立合作关系（继 Lilly、Pfizer、Novartis、argenx、BMS 之后第6家大药企），此前湿实验室评测证明 Chai 的模型无需靶点特异性训练即可找到结合子；估值已达 38 亿美元，融资规模达 4 亿美元。

Chai Discovery signed its 6th major pharma deal (with GSK) in 9 months following wet-lab validation; Chai-3 models found binders to all tested targets without target-specific training. Valuation now ~$3.8B.

**Insilico Medicine (Alex Zhavoronkov)** — 10月20日宣布与 Servier 的肿瘤学合作达到第一个里程碑（总价值最高 8.88 亿美元）；同期参加阿布扎比未来健康峰会（10月20-22日），其 AI 发现的肺纤维化候选药物 rentosertib 9月已在《Nature Biotechnology》发文，显示可逆转衰老标志物。

Insilico hit the first milestone in its $888M Servier oncology collaboration (Oct 20) and presented at Abu Dhabi Future Health Summit; their IPF drug rentosertib showed aging-marker reversal in 42 patients per a Sept Nature Biotechnology analysis.

## 大药企 AI 合作 · Big Pharma AI Deals

**AstraZeneca × insitro** — 10月15日签署合作协议：AstraZeneca 付 4000 万美元预付款，insitro 最高可获 14.6 亿美元里程碑付款及单位数版税；合作聚焦 MASH（代谢性脂肪性肝炎）和心肌病靶点发现，insitro 的"虚拟人体"AI 平台将利用实验室培育的细胞模型结合机器学习进行靶点发现。

AstraZeneca agreed to pay insitro $40M upfront plus up to $1.46B in milestones to apply insitro's "Virtual Human" AI platform toward MASH and cardiomyopathy target discovery (Oct 15).

Moderna will present Phase 3 melanoma data from its individualized mRNA neoantigen vaccine at ESMO Presidential Symposium on Oct 24; President Hoge noted AI accelerates mRNA design but can't compress trial timelines.
