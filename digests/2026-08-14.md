## AI 蛋白 & 基因组设计 | AI Protein & Genome Design

**Arc Institute（Brian Hie 团队）** — 8 月 6 日 *Science* 论文：利用基因组语言模型 Evo 1 & Evo 2，从零生成数千条噬菌体基因组，实验室验证其中 16 条为可活病毒——史上首次全 AI 生成的活病毒。作者同时呼吁：「生成 AI 编写病毒基因组的能力已然存在，约束它的治理框架却尚未建立。」 / Published Aug 6 in *Science*: using genome LMs Evo 1 & Evo 2, the team designed thousands of bacteriophage genomes; 16 proved viable in lab tests — the world's first AI-generated live viruses. Authors warned: "The ability to compose viral genomes using generative AI now exists; the governance to safely steer it does not."
