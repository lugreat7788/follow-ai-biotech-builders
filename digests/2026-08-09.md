# AI × 生物医药 Builder 日报 — 2026-08-09

## 概览 / Overview

Google DeepMind 的人才震荡持续发酵：Jeff Dean 离开 Google 27 年，与 Sanjay Ghemawat、Oriol Vinyals、Quoc Le 共同创立 **Discovery Loop**，专注 AI 自动化科学与工程研究（Google 为创始投资人）。与此同时，Demis Hassabis 继续担任 **Isomorphic Labs** CEO，后者正将首个 AI 设计的肿瘤候选药物 ISM8969 推向人体临床，并于 7 月联合发布一份涵盖 15+ 合作方的"生物韧性"（bioresilience）框架。AI 制药赛道资本与临床进展并行加速，本周关键词是：**从工具到临床**。

The DeepMind talent exodus deepens: Jeff Dean leaves Google after 27 years to co-found **Discovery Loop** alongside Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le, targeting AI automation of scientific and engineering research (with Google as a founding investor). Demis Hassabis — still CEO of **Isomorphic Labs** — is pushing its first AI-designed oncology drug ISM8969 toward human trials while the lab published a joint bioresilience framework with 15+ global partners. The defining theme this week: **from tools to clinic**.

## 🦠 AI 生物韧性 / AI Bioresilience (新专题)
