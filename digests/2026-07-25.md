# AI × 生物医药 Builder 日报 | 2026-07-25

## 概览 / Overview

本周 AI 生物医药赛道密度极高：Chai Discovery 以 4 亿美元 C 轮刷新估值至 38 亿美元，Moderna 完成泛肿瘤 mRNA-4200 一期首例入组，Jure Leskovec 团队的 Biomni 已成为全球超过 1 万个实验室的 AI 科学助手。Stef van Grieken（Cradle）与 GSK 等于 7 月 22 日联合创立业界首个抗体-抗原 AI 数据联盟，UW 蛋白质设计研究所同日宣布成立药物递送创新机构 I2D3，大动作持续。Demis Hassabis 的"FINRA for AI"监管框架建议在美政策圈持续发酵。

This week was dense across the AI × biomedical stack: Chai Discovery tripled its valuation with a $400M Series C; Moderna dosed its first patient in a pan-tumor mRNA cancer antigen trial; and Biomni (Leskovec, Stanford) became the most widely adopted AI co-scientist in biomedicine at 10,000+ labs. On July 22, Cradle (van Grieken) co-founded the Atlas Consortium—an industry-first antibody data-sharing initiative—while UW IPD launched I2D3 to bridge protein design with drug delivery. Demis Hassabis's FINRA-for-AI proposal continued gaining policy traction.

## AI 蛋白 / 分子设计 | AI Protein & Molecular Design

**Stef van Grieken（Cradle）** — 7 月 22 日，A-Alpha Bio 发起 Atlas Consortium，首创行业级抗体-抗原实验数据共享协作模型，Cradle 与 GSK、Boltz、Dyno Therapeutics 共同作为创始成员，正面解决 AI 蛋白工程的实验数据瓶颈。Van Grieken 表示："构建更优秀的蛋白质工程 AI 需要跨越从苗头发现到临床前阶段完整设计周期的实验数据。" / July 22: Cradle joins GSK, Boltz & Dyno Therapeutics as founding member of the Atlas Consortium—an industry-first antibody-antigen standardized data-sharing initiative targeting the experimental data bottleneck in AI protein engineering.

## AI 医学 / 研究工具 | AI Medicine & Research Tools

**Jure Leskovec（Stanford + Biomni）** — 7 月 11 日，Stanford 发布 Biomni——业界首个生物医学通用 AI 科学家（开源），可自动完成文献综述、假说生成、实验方案设计及数据分析，在 400+ 基准任务上达到资深科学家水平，已有逾 1 万家实验室使用，是生物医学领域迄今采用最广泛的 AI 科学助手。 / July 11: Biomni launches as the first general-purpose open-source biomedical AI co-scientist; benchmarked at senior-scientist level on 400+ tasks; 10,000+ labs now using it—the most widely adopted AI co-scientist in biomedicine.
- 来源：https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/07/biomni-ai-powered-biomedical-co-scientist
- 来源：https://techxplore.com/news/2026-07-biomni-ai-powered-biomedical-scientist.html

**Demis Hassabis（Google DeepMind）** — 发表长文《Frontier AI 新纪元框架》（约 7 月 14 日），主张 AGI"可能只需数年"，提议建立类 FINRA 的独立 AI 标准机构，对前沿模型进行发布前 30 天审查；Fortune 于 7 月 21 日刊发深度分析，指该提案在政策圈"正获得动力"。 / ~July 14: Published "A Framework for Frontier AI": AGI is "probably only a few short years away"; proposes FINRA-style independent body to screen frontier models 30 days pre-release. Fortune July 21 analysis: proposal "gaining momentum."
- 来源（原文）：https://demishassabis.substack.com/p/a-framework-for-frontier-ai-and-the-dawning-of-a-new-age
- 来源（Fortune）：https://fortune.com/2026/07/21/google-deepmind-ceo-demis-hassabis-finra-for-ai-proposal-gains-momentum-but-is-it-any-good/

**Eric Topol + Pranav Rajpurkar（Harvard）** — 7 月 13 日，两人联合在 npj Digital Medicine 发表《手术室里的人形机器人》框架论文，提出 embodied AI 进入手术场景的分阶段部署路线：从临床前验证到低风险辅助、再到高风险辅助，认为拟人化设计是机器人进入手术室的关键优势。 / July 13: Co-authored framework in npj Digital Medicine for staged OR integration of humanoid robots: pre-clinical validation → low-risk assistive → elevated-risk assistive; anthropomorphic design cited as key enabler.
- 来源：https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-02853-6

**Patrick Hsu（Arc Institute / Stanford）** — 约 7 月 22 日作为 Ground Truths 首位二次受邀嘉宾接受 Eric Topol 访谈（详见播客部分）；同期确认已加入 Stanford 病理学系。近期研究聚焦 Evo 生物基础模型、bridge RNA（"CRISPR 3.0"）及大语言生命模型（LLLMs）。 / ~July 22: First-ever repeat guest on Ground Truths (see Podcasts below); joined Stanford Pathology; recent work spans Evo foundation models, bridge RNA ("CRISPR 3.0"), and large language of life models.
