## 🔬 AI 蛋白 / 基因组设计 | AI Protein & Genome Design

**Mohammed AlQuraishi (Columbia University)** — 4 月于 *Nature Methods* 发表 ROCKET（Residual Optimization with Cryo-EM/ET Kinematic Template）方法：将 OpenFold（AlphaFold2 可训练复现版）的隐式结构知识作为概率先验，直接整合进 X 射线晶体学和冷冻电镜数据处理，突破了传统方法在侧链堆积和构象变化方面的瓶颈，实现结构解析精度的系统性提升。/ AlQuraishi Lab published ROCKET in *Nature Methods* (April 2026): integrates implicit structural knowledge from OpenFold directly as a statistical prior into cryo-EM, cryo-ET and X-ray crystallography experiments, enabling higher-accuracy structure determination for challenging conformational states.
- 来源：[AlphaFold as a prior – Nature Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03047-4) | [bioRxiv preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.02.18.638828v1)

**Arc Institute (Patrick Hsu Lab / Hie Lab)** — 虚拟细胞挑战赛 2026 明日（8 月 20 日）正式启动！这是 Arc 第二届全球赛事，考察各参赛团队的虚拟细胞模型能否准确预测细胞在未见上下文中基因活性的变化。上周（8/15–16），Arc 的 Proto 团队还参加了旧金山 re:AGENT hackathon，探索面向生物设计的 AI 科学家 agent。/ The 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge officially launches August 20 — Arc's second annual global competition evaluating virtual cell AI models on out-of-distribution cellular perturbations. Pre-registration and Discord are open now.
- 来源：[Arc Institute – Virtual Cell Challenge](https://arcinstitute.org/virtual-cell-initiative) | [GEN: Arc Institute Launches VCC](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/arc-institute-launches-virtual-cell-challenge-to-accelerate-ai-model-development/) | [virtualcellchallenge.org](https://virtualcellchallenge.org/)
