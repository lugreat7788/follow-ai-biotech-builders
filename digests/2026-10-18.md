# AI × 生物医药 Builder 日报 · 2026-10-18

## 概览 / Overview

本周最值得关注的三件事：Baker Lab 在 *Nature* 同日发表两篇从头设计蛋白酶的论文，首次实现 AI 设计的蛋白酶切割 TDP-43 等疾病靶点；Chai Discovery 拿下 GSK 合作，AI 零样本生成抗体绑定体通过实湿实验验证；Recursion 完成 2 亿美元超额认购增发并披露 Sanofi 里程碑，现金跑道延伸至 2029 年。

The week's three headlines: Baker Lab published two *Nature* papers on de novo-designed proteases targeting disease proteins like TDP-43; Chai Discovery landed a GSK collaboration after zero-shot wet-lab validation of AI-designed binders; and Recursion closed a $200M oversubscribed offering, extended its runway to 2029, and disclosed a Sanofi immunology milestone.

## 🧬 AI 蛋白 / 药物设计

**Institute for Protein Design（Baker Lab / @UWproteindesign）** — 2026-10-14 在 *Nature* 同日发表两篇论文：一篇报道 AI 从头设计的半胱氨酸蛋白酶（成功率 19%），另一篇报道 AI 设计的锌依赖金属蛋白酶（36% 催化活性），两类蛋白酶均可切割 TDP-43、淀粉样蛋白-β 和血清淀粉样蛋白 A 等疾病靶点。/ Baker Lab published two *Nature* papers on Oct 14 reporting AI-designed proteases—cysteine (19% success rate) and metalloprotease (36%)—that cleave disease-linked targets including TDP-43 and amyloid-β.
- 来源: [unite.ai](https://www.unite.ai/baker-lab-designs-ai-proteases-that-cleave-disease-linked-proteins/) · [Nature (cysteine paper)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11143-w) · [Cornell summary](https://necat.chem.cornell.edu/2026/10/15/ai-used-by-baker-lab-to-design-proteases/)

**Chai Discovery（@chaidiscovery）** — 2026-10-09 宣布与 GSK 合作，Chai-3 模型以零样本方式生成绑定体，GSK 内部湿实验验证所有测试靶点均成功，这是继 BMS、Lilly、Novartis、Pfizer 后 Chai 本年度第五家大药企合作。/ On Oct 9, Chai Discovery announced a collaboration with GSK: Chai-3 generated zero-shot binders to all tested targets, validated in GSK's wet lab—the company's fifth big pharma deal of 2026 after BMS, Lilly, Novartis, and Pfizer.
- 来源: [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/chai-discovery-announces-collaboration-with-gsk-following-successful-wet-lab-evaluation) · [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/gsk-drinks-chai-discoverys-ai-models-after-wet-lab-validation) · [Contract Pharma](https://www.contractpharma.com/breaking-news/chai-discovery-gsk-announce-collaboration-following-wet-lab-evaluation/)

**Alex Rives（@alexrives · Biohub）** — 入选 TIME100 AI 2026 榜单；今年早些时候发布 ESM Atlas（10 亿蛋白数据库）和 ESMFold2，现为 Biohub 科学负责人、MIT 助理教授。/ Named to TIME100 AI 2026; released ESM Atlas (1B protein database) and ESMFold2 earlier this year; now head of science at Biohub and assistant professor at MIT.
- 来源: [TIME100 AI 2026](https://time.com/collection/time100-ai/2026/alex-rives/) · [Broad Institute bio](https://www.broadinstitute.org/bios/alex-rives)
