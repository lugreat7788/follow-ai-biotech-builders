## 🔬 AI 蛋白与结构设计 | Protein & Structure Design

**Mohammed AlQuraishi / Columbia** — OpenFold3 持续推进（目标与 AlphaFold3 全面对齐）；4 月已在 Nature Methods 发表"AlphaFold as a prior"条件结构确定框架，探索侧链填装与结构系综建模等 AF3 尚未解决的难题。/ OpenFold3 continues toward full parity with AlphaFold3; April 2026 Nature Methods paper uses AlphaFold as a probabilistic prior for experimental structure determination.
- 来源: [AQ Lab Publications](https://www.aqlab.io/publications)

**Sergey Ovchinnikov / MIT** — MSA Pairformer 发表于 Cell（6 月），首个将 MSA 配对编码器扩展到蛋白质–蛋白质相互作用预测的紧凑语言模型，代码与 Colab 均已开源。/ MSA Pairformer published in Cell (June 2026) — compact MSA-based protein LM extending to protein–protein interactions, open-sourced with Colab.
- 来源: [MIT Biology / Ovchinnikov Lab](https://solab.org/)

**Jure Leskovec / Stanford — Biomni** — 7 月发布首个通用生物医学 AI Agent，可自动完成文献综述、假设生成、方案设计、生物信息学分析、临床推理等任务；已有超 10,000 个实验室使用，单个复杂分析任务从 3 周缩短到 35 分钟；开源 + 免费 Web 界面。/ Launched Biomni (July 2026): the first general-purpose biomedical AI co-scientist, adopted by 10,000+ labs, completing complex analyses in 35 min vs. 3 weeks; fully open-source.
- 来源: [Stanford Report](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/07/biomni-ai-powered-biomedical-co-scientist) · [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/science/article/3359945/meet-biomni-free-powerful-biomed-ai-agent-turning-data-hypotheses) · [AACR Blog](https://www.aacr.org/blog/2026/06/18/eye-on-ai-agentic-ai-brings-bots-to-the-bench/)

**Institute for Protein Design (UW)** — 7 月 22 日，UW 宣布成立 **I2D3**（AI 驱动药物设计与开发研究所），由药物化学、药代动力学、精神病学三位教授联合领衔，旨在打通 AI 蛋白设计到临床转化的全链路。/ July 22: UW launched I2D3 (Institute for AI-driven Drug Design & Development), bridging AI protein design to clinical translation across three UW departments.
