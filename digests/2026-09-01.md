## 💊 AI 癌症/制药突破 | AI Pharma Breakthroughs

**Moderna / Merck** — mRNA 个性化肿瘤疫苗 intismeran（V940/mRNA-4157）+帕博利珠单抗在 1,137 例黑色素瘤 Phase 3 试验（INTerpath-001，8月19日）中达到主终点 RFS 及关键次终点 DMFS，这是史上首个 mRNA 癌症疗法、也是首个个性化新抗原疗法的 Phase 3 成功；Moderna 股价当日大涨逾 120%，为公司有史以来最大单日涨幅。 / Intismeran + pembrolizumab met primary (RFS) and key secondary (DMFS) endpoints in Phase 3 melanoma trial (Aug 19) — the first-ever positive Phase 3 for an mRNA cancer therapy; Moderna shares surged >120%.
- 来源: [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/19/mrna-cancer-vaccine-trial-melanoma-merck-moderna/) | [BioPharm International](https://www.biopharminternational.com/view/merck-moderna-intismeran-autogene-pembrolizumab-phase-3-melanoma) | [AJMC](https://www.ajmc.com/view/moderna-merck-mrna-cancer-vaccine-succeeds-in-late-stage-trial)

**Recursion Pharmaceuticals** — Q2 财报（8月5日）：FDA 已批准 AI 设计 PI3Kα H1047R 抑制剂 REC-7735 的 IND，ZINNIA Phase 1/2 试验将于 2026 年下半年启动（从 242 个化合物到 IND 仅用 10 个月）；Genentech 将合作首个神经科学靶点推入早期发现项目；2026 年运营费用指引下调至 3.75 亿美元以下。 / Q2 results (Aug 5): IND cleared for AI-designed PI3Kα H1047R inhibitor REC-7735 (designed in 10 months, 242 compounds); Genentech's first neuroscience target advanced into early discovery.

## 🔬 AI 蛋白设计与计算生物 | AI Protein Design & Comp Bio

**Arc Institute** — 2026 虚拟细胞挑战赛（Virtual Cell Challenge）于 8月20日正式启动全体竞赛：零样本预测六种从未扰动过的细胞系对 CRISPRi 敲低的响应，大奖 10 万美元，NVIDIA、10x Genomics、Ultima Genomics 赞助；Brian Hie 实验室 Proto 团队参加了同期举办的 re:AGENT 黑客马拉松。 / 2026 Virtual Cell Challenge launched Aug 20: zero-shot prediction of CRISPRi knockdown responses in 6 unseen cell lines; $100K prize, sponsored by NVIDIA, 10x Genomics & Ultima Genomics.
- 来源: [Arc Institute](https://arcinstitute.org/news/virtual-cell-challenge-2026)

**Alexander Rives / Chan Zuckerberg Biohub** — 5月下旬发布蛋白质生物学世界模型（ESMFold2 + ESMC + ESM Atlas）：ESMC 以 28 亿序列训练，ESMFold2 可预测蛋白质及复合物结构，ESM Atlas 索引 68 亿蛋白质及 11 亿结构；全套 MIT 许可开源，Rives 已转任 Biohub 科学主任。 / Biohub released protein biology world model (ESMC + ESMFold2 + ESM Atlas, May 2026): trained on 2.8B sequences; 6.8B proteins in ESM Atlas; MIT-licensed open source.
- 来源: [Biohub News](https://biohub.org/news/world-model-of-protein-biology/) | [GEN](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/biohub-releases-protein-biology-world-model-to-address-disease/) | [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/esmfold2)

**Sergey Ovchinnikov (MIT)** — 7月发表预印本，将蛋白质语言模型扩展到蛋白质–蛋白质相互作用预测（MSA Pairformer），同月另有关于小域蛋白折叠稳定性预测的研究。 / July preprint: extending protein language models to protein-protein interaction prediction with MSA Pairformer; also published work on accurate stability prediction for small protein domains.
- 来源: [ResearchGate – Ovchinnikov](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Sergey-Ovchinnikov)

**Simon Kohl / Latent Labs** — 3月发布预印本「Latent-Y」，提出一个经实验室湿实验验证的自主 agent，将生成式 AI 与闭环实验反馈结合用于从头药物设计。 / March preprint: Latent-Y, a lab-validated autonomous agent for de novo drug design that couples generative AI with wet-lab experimental feedback loops.
- 来源: [Crunchbase – Simon Kohl](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/simon-kohl)

**Institute for Protein Design (UW / David Baker)** — 华盛顿研究基金会投入 700 万美元支持 Baker 实验室 AI 驱动酶设计四年计划，目标教育新科学家、加速发现并推动成果产业化；另有 Audacious Project 4500 万美元慈善资助支撑。 / Washington Research Foundation committed $7M for AI-enabled enzyme design at UW IPD (4-year initiative); Audacious Project's $45M philanthropic grant also in place.
- 来源: [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/from-computer-to-lab-to-market-nobel-winner-david-baker-lands-7m-for-new-protein-program/) | [WRF](https://www.wrfseattle.org/news/washington-research-foundation-awards-7-million-to-uw-institute-for-protein-design-for-ambitious-initiative-to-advance-ai-enabled-enzyme-design/)
