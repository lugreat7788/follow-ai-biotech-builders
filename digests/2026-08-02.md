**Demis Hassabis / Google DeepMind** — AlphaFold 专属团队于 7 月 29 日正式解散，团队成员分流至 Gemini 项目、Isomorphic Labs 等，John Jumper、Jonas Adler、Alexander Pritzel 等核心作者已加入 Anthropic；Hassabis 同日提议建立美国主导的 AI 全球监管机构（类 FINRA 模式），要求前沿模型发布前 30 天提交安全审查。/ The dedicated AlphaFold team was disbanded on July 29; Jumper and two co-authors joined Anthropic. **Alexander Rives / Chan Zuckerberg Biohub** — 5 月 27 日发布 ESMFold2 + ESMC 蛋白世界模型：6B 参数、68 亿条序列，可在秒级预测结构并设计抗体；五个癌症/免疫靶点（EGFR、PD-L1、CTLA-4 等）的迷你蛋白设计成功率达 36–88%，Biohub 同步宣布 5 亿美元 Virtual Biology Initiative。/ Released ESMFold2 (May 27) — a 6B-parameter protein world model yielding 36–88% hit rates on five cancer/immunology targets in days rather than years; Biohub's $500M Virtual Biology Initiative simultaneously announced.

**Simon Kohl / Latent Labs** — 发布 Latent-Y，全球首个自主生物制剂设计 AI Agent，在 9 个靶点中产出 6 个确认结合物（67% 成功率，单位数纳摩尔亲和力），完成时间比专家估计快 56 倍；已在 SynBioBeta 2026 上展示。/ Released Latent-Y, the world's first autonomous biologics design AI agent: 67% hit rate across 9 targets, single-digit nM affinities, 56× faster than expert estimates.
- 来源: [SynBioBeta](https://www.synbiobeta.com/read/inside-latent-labs-autonomous-drug-design-engine) · [SynBioBeta Speaker](https://www.syntheticbiologysummit.com/2026-speakers/simon-kohl)

**Sergey Ovchinnikov / MIT** — 7 月发表 MSA Pairformer 论文，将蛋白语言建模扩展至蛋白质–蛋白质相互作用领域，是对 AlphaFold-Multimer 的方法论补充。/ Published MSA Pairformer (July 2026) — extending protein language modeling to protein–protein interactions.
