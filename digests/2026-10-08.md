## AI 制药公司动态 / AI Pharma Company News

**Profluent** — BioMarin 与 Profluent 启动合作，利用 Profluent 蛋白语言模型（含超 1150 亿序列的 Profluent Protein Atlas）设计并优化酶疗法候选分子，BioMarin 负责筛选并推进临床前测试（2026-10-01）。
BioMarin has kicked off a research collaboration with Profluent to use its protein language models to generate and optimise enzyme-therapy candidates; BioMarin will screen outputs and advance selected candidates into preclinical testing.
— Source: [Pharmaceutical Technology, 2026-10-01](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/newsletters/biomarin-profluent-ai-enzyme-therapies)

**Insilico Medicine（Alex Zhavoronkov）** — Insilico 提名 ISM1354 为临床前候选药物：一款由 Chemistry42 平台 AI 设计的 GIPR 拮抗剂，靶向肥胖及 2 型糖尿病，多物种口服生物利用度高达 104%，与 GLP-1 药物联用预期可减少脂肪同时保留肌肉量（2026-09-30 / 2026-10-02 报道）。
Insilico nominated ISM1354, an AI-designed small-molecule GIPR antagonist, as a preclinical candidate for obesity and type 2 diabetes; the compound showed oral bioavailability up to 104% across multiple species and is designed to complement GLP-1 drugs by preserving muscle mass.
— Source: [Drug Target Review, 2026-10-02](https://www.drugtargetreview.com/news/insilico-medicine-nominates-ai-designed-ism1354-gipr-antagonist-for-obesity/2136626.article) | [Technode Global](https://technode.global/?p=994109)

## AI 蛋白 / 生物基础模型 / AI Protein & Bio Foundation Models

**Arc Institute** — Arc Institute 于 2026-10-05 发布 PIE（Perturbation-Informed Estimator）模型，整合精心整理的生物学知识，可预测细胞在从未被测试过的条件下的响应，支持虚拟细胞挑战赛（Virtual Cell Challenge，最终测试集 2026-10-22 发布）。
Arc Institute released PIE on October 5, a model that draws on curated biological knowledge to predict how cells respond to conditions they have never been tested in — directly relevant to the ongoing Virtual Cell Challenge (final test set drops October 22).
— Source: [Arc Institute Newsroom, 2026-10-05](https://arcinstitute.org/news) | [Virtual Cell Challenge](https://virtualcellchallenge.org/)

## 行业洞察 / Industry Insights

**虚拟药企 / Virtual Biotech (37,000 AI Agents)** — 一项研究让 37,000 个 AI agent 回顾历史临床试验，发现靶向作用谱较窄（narrowly active）的药物成功率更高，为 AI 靶点选择策略提供了数据支撑（约 2026-10-05）。
A virtual biotech run by 37,000 AI agents combed past drug trials and found that drugs targeting narrowly active proteins are more likely to succeed — providing an empirical signal for AI-driven target selection strategies.
— Source: [Earth.com, ~2026-10-05](https://earth.com/science/virtual-biotech-staffed-by-37000-ai-agents-found-a-marker-for-more-successful-medicines)

**抗体可成药性联盟 / Antibody Developability AI Consortium** — 行业联盟宣布启动 AI 驱动的抗体可成药性预测项目，构建一个涵盖 10,000 条序列的标准化数据集，旨在建立可比较的行业基准（2026-10-01）。
An industry consortium announced an AI-driven antibody developability prediction initiative, building a standardized 10,000-sequence dataset to establish comparable benchmarks across the field.
— Source: [Drug Target Review, 2026-10-01](https://www.drugtargetreview.com/)
