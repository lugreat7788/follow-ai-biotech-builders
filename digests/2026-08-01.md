## 四、AI 医疗 (AI Medicine)

**Eric Topol / NOHARM 研究** — 7 月 29 日 Fortune 报道：斯坦福、哈佛等机构 NOHARM 基准研究对 OpenEvidence、OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol、Anthropic Claude Fable 5、Doximity Ask 进行 1100 个临床案例测试，获 13,000 条医师标注。关键发现：**76.6% 的有害错误为遗漏（omission）而非错误陈述**，医生无法通过事实核查捕捉此类错误；Doximity Ask 综合表现最优，但方法论存争议。/ A July 29 Fortune report covers the NOHARM benchmark (Stanford/Harvard/ARISE): 1,100 clinical cases tested against OpenEvidence, GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Fable 5, and Doximity Ask. Key finding: 76.6% of harmful errors were omissions — information left out, which physicians cannot fact-check unless they already know to look for it. Doximity Ask ranked highest overall, though methodology is disputed.
